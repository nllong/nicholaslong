I have always enjoyed learning. I went to school for Electrical Engineering and Architectural Engineering. I have a PhD and a Master's degree from the University of Colorado Boulder in Architectural Engineering (building sciences). I work at a National Lab, where I manage the Building, District, and Industrial Modeling Group, serve as the geothermal subprogram lead for pilots and demonstrations, and work as a senior researcher focused on buildings, districts, and industrial modeling and analysis. Much of my work centers on district energy systems, including leading URBANopt District Energy Systems and projects that pair geothermal borehole thermal energy storage with district-scale energy modeling.

Find more about my vocation on the contact page below.

I enjoy hanging with the family, rock climbing, playing the bass guitar and piano, yoga, running, and working on anything mechanical.
